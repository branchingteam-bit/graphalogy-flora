Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the first analysis really free?

Yes, completely. No credit card, no commitment. Flora believes everyone deserves to understand themselves better.

How do I prepare for my analysis?

Just write a few paragraphs on unlined paper using your usual pen. Don't try to write neatly — Flora wants to see your natural handwriting.

Are sessions online or in-person?

Flora works with clients both in-person in Dubai and online worldwide. She can analyse a photo of your handwriting.

How old do you need to be for analysis?

Flora does not do full personality analysis for children under 18 (traits are still developing), but she does offer the kids writing program for ages 6–17.

How long does a program take per day?

Just 10–15 minutes of intentional writing daily. Simple, consistent, and powerful — even with a busy schedule.

Does graphotherapy really work?

Yes — it is backed by neuroscience. UCLA research shows that changing how you move your fingers stimulates different parts of the brain and produces measurable chemical changes.
