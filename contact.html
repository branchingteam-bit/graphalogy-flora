Common questions

Before you get in touch

Is the first analysis really free?

Yes, completely. No card, no commitment. Flora believes everyone should get to understand themselves a little better.

How do I prepare?

Write a few paragraphs on unlined paper with your usual pen. Don’t aim for neat. Flora needs your natural handwriting.

Online or in person?

Both. Flora works in person in Dubai and online worldwide, and can read a clear photo of your handwriting.

Is there an age limit?

Full personality analysis is for adults 18 and over, as traits are still forming before then. Children aged 6 to 17 are welcome on the kids writing program.

How much time does a program take?

About ten to fifteen minutes of intentional writing a day. Manageable alongside a full schedule.

Does grapho-therapy actually work?

It is grounded in neuroscience. UCLA research found that changing how you move a finger activates different parts of the brain and produces measurable chemical changes.
