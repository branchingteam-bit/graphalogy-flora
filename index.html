How working with Flora goes

  1. 1

    Free analysis

    You send a sample of your natural handwriting. Flora reads it and tells you what she sees. No charge, no commitment.

  2. 2

    Your plan

    Together you choose a focus. Flora designs writing exercises aimed at your specific traits.

  3. 3

    Daily practice

    Ten to fifteen minutes of intentional writing a day. Simple, consistent, quietly powerful.

  4. 4

    Review

    Flora compares your first and latest pages to measure the shift, then decides what comes next.
