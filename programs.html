The process

How every program runs

  1. 1

    Analysis

    Your handwriting is read to identify your specific traits, patterns and the areas worth working on.

  2. 2

    Program design

    Flora builds personalised writing exercises aimed squarely at those traits.

  3. 3

    Daily practice

    10–15 minutes of intentional writing each day. Simple, consistent, repeated.

  4. 4

    Review

    Flora compares your first and last samples to measure the change and decide what comes next.

Kids writing program

For children aged 6–17

More than handwriting — the foundation for confident learning. It uses structured handwriting and grapho-therapy-based exercises (an approach called MPNL) to work on specific strokes and patterns, supporting the connection between brain, hand, cognition and behaviour.

Through intentional practice, children build skills that support focus, memory, cognitive development, emotional regulation and self-expression — with visibly better handwriting along the way.

  • Focus & concentration
  • Memory retention & cognitive skills

Signature Analysis & Correction

Through signature analysis — what your signature says about you — and intentional correction, you gain a clearer sense of how you present yourself, and how small changes to your signing style can support a more confident, aligned self-image.

Your signature is the one piece of handwriting you consciously craft, and yet it reveals your subconscious self-image more honestly than almost any other mark.

  • What your current signature says about your self-image
  • The patterns Flora identifies in it
  • Personalised exercises to move it toward confidence
  • Particularly valuable for young adults, professionals and entrepreneurs
