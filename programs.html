How Graphotherapy Works

Every program follows the same structured process — beginning with understanding, followed by transformation.

📋

📈

Flora compares your first and last samples to measure change and decide next steps.

Signature Analysis & Correction

Through Signature Analysis (what your signature says about you) and intentional signature correction, you can gain deeper awareness of your personal expression and explore how small changes in your signing style can support a more confident and aligned self-image.

Your signature is the one piece of handwriting you consciously craft — and yet it reveals your subconscious self-image more honestly than almost any other mark.

  • Reveals your self-image and how you want to be perceived
  • Flora analyses the current signature and identifies patterns
  • Personalised exercises to shift your signature toward confidence
  • Particularly valuable for young adults, professionals, and entrepreneurs

Your Signature Is Your Statement
