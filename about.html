The Science

What is Graphology?

GRAPHOLOGY is the study of handwriting — a theory and practice for inferring a person's character, disposition and attitudes from their handwriting. Every stroke, space, rhythm and movement in handwriting is shaped by the intricate connection between the brain, nervous system and hand.

An important aspect of Graphology is Grapho-therapy — the scientific way of writing to enhance the positivity of our personality. Research by UCLA found that changing the movement of just one finger stimulated a totally separate part of the brain and produced different chemicals, validating why even a tiny shift in how you write can trigger measurable neurological and behavioral change.

Handwriting Analysis identifies traits, not moods or temporary emotions. If a trait shows in handwriting, it has become a natural reflex — the person may not even be aware of it. This is what makes graphology such a powerful tool for self-awareness and change.
