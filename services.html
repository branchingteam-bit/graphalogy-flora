Behind the programs

What the work draws on

These aren’t separate things to buy. They’re the parts of the practice that every program is built from.

Handwriting analysis

The starting point for everything. Handwriting is often described as a reflection of your brain’s blueprint. Like a medical report, it doesn’t lie. It identifies traits, not moods: more than 120 of them, read from a single sample, covering emotional resilience, self-esteem, fears and how you think. The first analysis is always free.

Grapho-therapy

How the change actually happens. Rewriting letters builds new muscle memory and, with repetition, a new reflex. UCLA research found that changing the movement of a single finger activated a separate part of the brain and produced different neurochemicals, which is why a small change, like moving a t-bar, can register as a real neurological shift.

Signature work

Your signature holds three things at once: who you are, who you believe you are, and who you want others to see. A strong one has a clear standalone capital, letters that reflect self-worth, and a single underline rising around 45 degrees. Small, intentional corrections move it toward confidence.

Health in Handwriting

Prevention-focused work that treats writing as a doorway rather than the problem to fix. A daily, physical practice that helps the body and mind draw on their own capacity to heal. This is the basis of the 90-day program.

Workshops & training

Group sessions and one-to-one mentoring on graphology and self-improvement through writing, for schools, teams and private groups. In person in Dubai or online. Practical and interactive, with tools you can use the same week.

Career guidance

Handwriting shows your natural thinking style, creativity, leadership tendencies and attention to detail. Flora uses that reading to help you move toward work that genuinely suits you. Useful for students, career changers and anyone feeling stuck.
